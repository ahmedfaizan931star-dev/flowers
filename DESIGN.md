# Petallique Design System Contract

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Petallique represents a soft, premium, editorial lifestyle brand centered on bespoke, ethical floristry. It relies heavily on high contrast asymmetric layouts, extensive custom breathing whitespace, and elegant classical-meets-modern typography to separate it from fast-commodity flower marketplaces.

## Color System Roles
Every color has been strictly bounded to semantic application parameters:
- **Background (`--bg`)**: `#FAF8F5` - Soft, warm sandstone/alabaster tone simulating heavy premium paper pulp.
- **Surface (`--surface`)**: `#FFFFFF` - Clean white background of cards, menus, and forms.
- **Surface-2 (`--surface-2`)**: `#F4EFEA` - Elevated tone for container highlights and interactive canvas steps.
- **Border (`--border`)**: `#E6DFD5` - Elegant muted warm line dividing structure without causing layout noise.
- **Text/Charcoal (`--text`)**: `#241E1C` - High contrast espresso-black readable type for clean layout.
- **Muted (`--muted`)**: `#6D625D` - Balanced gray-brown for captions, meta tags, and placeholder titles.
- **Primary Accent (`--primary`)**: `#9C4153` - Velvet dusty rose tone for critical headers, counts, buttons, and gold highlights.
- **Accent Sage (`--accent`)**: `#4A5F4B` - Botanical sage green to complement floral curation aesthetics.
- **Champagne Gold (`--gold`)**: `#C29F5D` - High luxury metal color representing seasonal exclusivity.

## Typography Rules
- **Display Serif Font Stack**: `Cormorant Garamond`, Georgia, serif.
  - High stroke contrast, sophisticated italic, and elegant letter spacing.
- **Sans-Serif Font Stack**: `Inter`, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
  - High geometric precision, high readability on small labels, counters, and quantity values.
- **Line Heights**: Headings `1.2`, Body `1.6` to allow breathing space.

## Layout Principles
- **12-column grid system** for desktop showcases.
- **Asymmetry is prioritized**: highlighted central cards receive negative translating transforms to disrupt monotony.
- **Interactive Slide-out drawers** used for premium basket management to keep checkout pathways clear.
- **Fluid typography clamps** to scale elements dynamically from 375px mobile displays to high-resolution desktop viewports.
