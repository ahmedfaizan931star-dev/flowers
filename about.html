Our Heritage

The Philosophy of Poetic Botany

An exploration of organic symmetry, sustainable harvesting techniques, and the master florists committed to nature's velvet craft.

Grown with patience,
arranged with couture care.

Established by creative botanists, Petallique emerged out of a desire to rescue floral presentation from standard generic wire structures and pesticide-laden global shipments. We believe a bouquet shouldn't just be decoration; it represents a breathing canvas.

Each dawn, our team inspects our biodynamic cooperative yields, securing only the stalks that show absolute health. We respect structural movement, utilizing organic twine wrapping and specialized moisture pads to preserve the flower's native visual posture during courier dispatch.

Caring for your living art

Always use lukewarm water. Before placing the stems into your vase, slice exactly 2cm off each branch at a strict 45-degree angle. This opens capillaries, allowing optimal water absorption. Replace fluid every 48 hours.
Avoid direct sunlight, draft avenues, and central heating devices. Flowers love fresh, humid air. Keep arrangements away from ripening fruits like bananas, as they emit ethylene gas which accelerates aging.
Pluck off lower leaves that would otherwise submerge under the water level to prevent bacterial growth. As some outer petals look withered, gently peel them away—this is normal and allows internal core petals to swell and expand fully.
